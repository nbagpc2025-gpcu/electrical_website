About the records on this page

This page draws on the supplied Alumni performance and connect report (8 pages) and the Electrical Department achievements presentation (17 slides). The directory includes 17 listed alumni plus a separate 1999-batch success story. Career groups are classifications of those records, not department-wide employment rates.

The placement chart uses the presentation’s underlying counts to distinguish jobs from higher studies. Planned events, journal submissions and source-reported journal rankings are labelled accordingly. No unsupported salary, donation amount, current ranking or carbon-saving estimate is added. Photos come from the supplied documents. Original documents are retained privately rather than republished with internal screenshots.
