Useful Portals

Official portals students, staff and employers use most: admissions, scholarships, learning, internships and jobs.

Government Polytechnic, Chhotaudepur functions under the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Government of Gujarat. Below are the official portals students, staff and employers use most.

PortalWebsiteUsed for
Government Polytechnic, Chhotaudepurwww.gpchhotaudepur.ac.in

  • Details of departments, courses, intake and faculty
  • Fee structure, admission information and contact numbers

Chhota Udepur Districtchhotaudepur.nic.in
  • District administration notices and government orders

2. Regulators & Parent Bodies

PortalWebsiteUsed for
AICTEaicte.gov.in
  • Annual approval (EoA) of the institute and courses
  • Approval process handbook, norms and regulations
  • Schemes: Pragati, Saksham, MODROBS, faculty development, internships
  • Circulars, academic calendar and model curriculum
Gujarat Technological University (GTU)gtu.ac.in
  • Syllabus and curriculum for every diploma branch and semester
  • Exam schedule, hall tickets, results and re-assessment
  • Circulars on enrolment, fees and academic rules
  • Transcripts, degree/diploma certificates and verification
Commissionerate of Technical Education, Gujaratcteguj.in
  • State orders and circulars for government polytechnics
  • Websites of all government polytechnics
  • Staff matters: recruitment, transfers, promotions
  • State schemes and grants for technical institutes
Education Department, Govt. of Gujaratgujarat-education.gov.in
  • Government Resolutions (GRs) and policies on education
  • Scheme guidelines (MYSY, SSIP etc.)
  • Department notifications and contacts
Knowledge Consortium of Gujarat (KCG)kcg.gujarat.gov.in
  • Faculty training and capacity-building programmes
  • Student programmes, competitions and workshops
  • E-resources and online learning content

3. Students – Admission

PortalWebsiteUsed for
ACPDC (Diploma Admission Committee)acpdc.gujarat.gov.in
  • Admission rules, eligibility and reservation policy
  • Admission schedule, key dates and notices
  • Provisional and final merit lists, cut-offs of previous years
  • List of institutes, seats, fees and help centres; FAQs
ACPDC Online Registrationgujdiploma.admissions.nic.in
  • Online registration for first-year diploma after Std 10
  • C2D (ITI to Diploma) lateral entry registration
  • Document upload, fee payment and choice filling
  • Seat allotment result and admission confirmation
ACPC Gujaratacpc.gujarat.gov.in
  • Diploma-to-Degree (D2D) admission to engineering degree
  • Merit list, choice filling and allotment for D2D
  • Admission rules and notices for degree courses

4. Students – Scholarships & Financial Aid

PortalWebsiteUsed for
MYSY Scholarshipmysy.gujarat.gov.in
  • Fresh and renewal applications for MYSY scholarship
  • Tuition fee assistance (up to ₹25,000 per year for diploma), hostel and book aid
  • Eligibility: 80+ percentile in Std 10, family income up to ₹6 lakh
  • Application status and help-centre document verification
Digital Gujaratdigitalgujarat.gov.in
  • Post-matric scholarships for ST, SC, OBC/SEBC and EWS students
  • Very important for tribal (ST) students of Chhota Udepur
  • Income, caste and other certificates online
  • Track application status; resubmit when sent back by institute
National Scholarship Portalscholarships.gov.in
  • Central government scholarships for diploma students
  • AICTE Pragati (girls) and Saksham (specially-abled) schemes
  • Institute (INO) verification of student applications
  • Payment status through Aadhaar-linked bank account
PM Vidyalaxmipmvidyalaxmi.co.in
  • Single-window application for education loans from banks
  • Interest subvention / subsidy schemes
  • Track loan application status

5. Students – Learning, Records & Certificates

PortalWebsiteUsed for
SWAYAMswayam.gov.in
  • Free online courses by IITs, NITTTRs and universities
  • Earn certificates and credits (MOOCs)
  • Self-learning for subjects in the diploma syllabus
NPTELnptel.ac.in
  • Free engineering video lectures in all branches
  • Proctored certification exams
  • Useful for students and faculty upskilling
National Digital Library of Indiandl.iitkgp.ac.in
  • Free e-books, notes and question papers
  • Study material in English, Hindi and Gujarati
  • Research and reference material for projects
DigiLockerdigilocker.gov.in
  • Digital copies of marksheets and certificates
  • Aadhaar, PAN and other documents in one place
  • Valid as original documents for admission and jobs
Academic Bank of Credits (APAAR)abc.gov.in
  • Create APAAR ID (One Nation One Student ID)
  • Store academic credits earned from courses
  • Credit transfer under NEP 2020
Anti-Raggingantiragging.in
  • Mandatory online anti-ragging undertaking by students and parents
  • Register complaints about ragging
  • UGC/AICTE anti-ragging regulations and helpline

6. Students – Internships, Apprenticeships & Jobs

PortalWebsiteUsed for
AICTE Internship Portalinternship.aicte-india.org
  • Find internships in companies and government bodies
  • Mandatory internship for diploma students under AICTE curriculum
  • Apply online and get internship certificate
NATSnats.education.gov.in
  • One-year paid apprenticeship for diploma holders
  • Monthly stipend shared by Government of India
  • Proficiency certificate valued by employers
  • Institute registration to link students with industry
Apprenticeship India (NAPS)apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
  • Apprenticeship opportunities across trades and industries
  • Online contract registration and stipend support
  • Search establishments offering apprenticeships
PM Internship Schemepminternship.mca.gov.in
  • 12-month internships in top companies of India
  • Monthly assistance and one-time grant
  • Open to eligible diploma holders (age and income criteria)
Anubandham Gujaratanubandham.gujarat.gov.in
  • Gujarat state employment portal for job seekers
  • Online job fairs (Rozgar Bharti Melas)
  • Mukhyamantri Apprenticeship Yojana openings
  • Register with Employment Exchange online
National Career Servicencs.gov.in
  • Private and government job listings across India
  • Career counselling and skill assessment
  • Job fairs and online interviews
OJAS Gujaratojas.gujarat.gov.in
  • Online applications for Gujarat government jobs
  • Download call letters and check results
  • Recruitment notifications of many departments
GSSSB / GPSCgsssb.gujarat.gov.in / gpsc.gujarat.gov.in
  • Class 3 (GSSSB) and Class 1–2 (GPSC) recruitment exams
  • Technical posts for diploma holders (e.g. Additional Assistant Engineer)
  • Exam syllabus, answer keys and results

MoE Innovation Cellmic.gov.in

Startup Indiastartupindia.gov.in

  • Free learning courses on entrepreneurship

9. Industry & Employers

PortalWebsiteUsed for
Udyam Registrationudyamregistration.gov.in
  • Free MSME registration (Udyam certificate)
  • Access to MSME schemes, loans and subsidies
  • Useful for alumni starting their own business
Industries Commissionerate, Gujaratic.gujarat.gov.in
  • Industrial policies and incentive schemes of Gujarat
  • District Industries Centre (DIC) services
  • Self-employment and entrepreneurship schemes
GIDCgidc.gujarat.gov.in

Skill India Digital Hubskillindiadigital.gov.in

Anubandham (employer)anubandham.gujarat.gov.in
