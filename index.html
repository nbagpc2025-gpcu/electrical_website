Electrical Engineering Department

The department has offered a three-year diploma in Electrical Engineering since 1994, with the aim of producing capable engineers for society. Its qualified faculty teach and carry out research in electrical engineering, and its laboratories give students hands-on practice on machines, power systems, measurement and electronics.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision & Mission

visibility

Vision

“To transform learners in to best technocrat in field of electrical engineering for collective alliance benefit of industry, society and government”
Mission
M-1

Provide quality education by ensuring optimal use of available resources to produce skilled technical personnel in the field of electrical engineering

M-2

Implant various technical and soft skills to enrich their problem solving and project management skills

M-3

Promote organizational and leadership qualities in learners for employability, start ups and Entrepreneurship through various extra-curricular and co-curricular activities

M-4

Create an environment where technical personnel will become lifelong learner and ethical servant of Society

M-5

Develop alliance with industries and other such institute to improve teaching learning, Skill development and placement
